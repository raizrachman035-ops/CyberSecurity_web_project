## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a premier enterprise digital posture for Indonesia’s fast-scaling technological and institutional sectors. The aesthetic is modern, authoritative, and frictionless—marrying high-level corporate governance with the forward-looking agility of modern software platforms. 

The visual style is rooted in **Corporate Modernism with Architectural Structure**:
- **Clarity over decorative excess:** High content density delivered through razor-sharp hierarchy, strict vertical rhythm, and ample negative space.
- **Deep institutional trust:** Grounded by authoritative deep maritime navy hues balanced against high-impact cyan and royal blue accents that denote precision, speed, and intelligence.
- **Micro-elevation & hairline discipline:** Tactile feel achieved through crisp 1px borders, subtle surface offsets, and soft diffused shadows rather than heavy skeuomorphism or novelty trends.
- **Target Audience:** C-suite executives, enterprise procurement directors, public sector digital transformation leaders, and engineering management across Southeast Asia.
- **Emotional Resonance:** Unwavering reliability, technological prestige, operational clarity, and forward-looking momentum.

## Colors

The palette establishes an immediate sense of sovereign security and digital sophistication.

- **Primary (`#0F2942`)**: Deep Maritime Navy. Used for authoritative anchor elements, primary action states, key navigation structures, and high-impact structural containers.
- **Secondary (`#2563EB`)**: Royal Blue. Drives interactive focus, primary CTAs, active indicator rings, link highlights, and state changes requiring prompt engagement.
- **Tertiary (`#1E6091`)**: Trust Cyan / Slate Blue. Serves as a bridge between the deep navy and royal blue, supporting secondary actions, badge backgrounds, subtle charts, and informational indicators.
- **Neutral (`#64748B`)**: Balanced Slate. Forms the bedrock for metadata, tertiary iconography, supportive captions, and quiet structural dividers.
- **Base Surfaces & Backgrounds**:
  - `surface-canvas`: `#F8FAFC` (Clean, warm-tinted cool gray canvas that reduces eye strain compared to harsh `#FFFFFF`).
  - `surface-card`: `#FFFFFF` (Pure white for raised cards, dashboards, and interactive inputs).
  - `surface-subtle`: `#F1F5F9` (Subtle container fill for data tables, nested cards, and input backgrounds).
  - `border-hairline`: `#E2E8F0` (Default subtle structural separator).
  - `text-strong`: `#0F172A` (Dark slate, providing WCAG AAA compliant text contrast against pure white).
  - `text-muted`: `#475569` (Body reading tier).

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy is engineered for bilingual precision (Bahasa Indonesia and English), demanding equal legibility across long-form enterprise documentation, dense data analytics, and high-impact marketing declarations.

- **Primary Typeface (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Provides a contemporary, confident, and sculpted geometric feel. Its humanist apertures keep large-format copy engaging while retaining disciplined posture in body contexts.
- **Technical & Utility Typeface (`Inter`)**: Applied exclusively to labels, data headers, table metrics, and micro-badges to leverage its neutral, ultra-legible tabular figures and tight tracking.
- **Tight Kerning on Displays**: Display and headline tiers employ negative letter tracking to eliminate loose negative space in uppercase and title cases, conveying institutional authority.
- **Fluid Scaling**: Headlines scale down automatically on mobile viewports below 768px to ensure long compound Indonesian business terms do not create awkward hyphenation or wrapping breaks.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an 8pt architectural rhythm within a structured 12-column grid, prioritizing clear data modularity and controlled horizontal scanning paths.

- **Grid Systems**:
  - **Desktop (≥ 1280px)**: 12-column fluid grid, max container width `1280px` (or `1440px` for dense enterprise cockpits), with a default `2.5rem` canvas margin and `1.5rem` column gutters.
  - **Tablet (768px – 1279px)**: 8-column layout with `1.5rem` outer margins and `1.25rem` gutters. Reflow multi-column analytics into 2-up grids.
  - **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column layout with `1rem` margin and `1rem` gutters. Full-width stacked elements with pinned or bottom-anchored action points.
- **Spacing Application Rules**:
  - `space-xs` (4px): Micro gaps between icons and badge text; grouped micro-labels.
  - `space-sm` (8px): Internal form control paddings; sibling button gaps in button groups.
  - `space-md` (16px): Standard gap between form fields; padding inside cards on mobile; list row spacing.
  - `space-lg` (24px): Standard internal padding for enterprise cards, dialogue boxes, and module headers.
  - `space-xl` (40px): Section breaks, stack offsets between unrelated card groupings, and hero content spacing.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses a refined hybrid of **low-contrast borders** and **ambient cool-tinted shadows**. Heavy dropshadows are forbidden; surfaces feel machined and physical through subtle surface value shifts.

- **Layer 0 (Canvas Base)**: `#F8FAFC`. The foundational backdrop for all page views.
- **Layer 1 (Card & Modular Paneling)**: `#FFFFFF` surfaced with a crisp 1px outline in `#E2E8F0` and an ambient shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 41, 66, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 41, 66, 0.02)`.
- **Layer 2 (Interactive Flyouts & Hover States)**: `#FFFFFF` with `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(15, 41, 66, 0.06), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(15, 41, 66, 0.04)` and border `#CBD5E1`.
- **Layer 3 (Modals & Overlays)**: `#FFFFFF` elevated with `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 41, 66, 0.10), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 41, 66, 0.06)`, framed against a backdrop veil of `#0F2942` at 40% opacity with a `4px` backdrop blur.
- **Surface Elevation Contrast**: Sub-containers within white cards (such as code displays, metrics summaries, or summary footers) invert to `#F1F5F9` with no shadow and an internal `1px` border of `#E2E8F0`.

## Shapes

The design system maintains a balanced, professional corner curvature (`roundedness: 2`), projecting technical modernism without the sterile aggression of sharp 90-degree corners or the casual playfulness of full pills.

- **Micro Components (Chips, Tags, Badges)**: Fixed `0.375rem` (6px) or soft pill (`9999px`) purely for operational status tags.
- **Inputs & Buttons (`0.5rem` / 8px)**: Consistent tactile form controls that fit neatly into enterprise data entry rows.
- **Standard Cards & Modals (`0.75rem` to `1rem` / 12px to 16px)**: Exterior corners of containers provide structured separation from canvas elements.
- **Structural Framing**: Outlines adhere to an exact `1px` stroke width. Dynamic focus states apply a double-ring mechanism (2px solid `#2563EB` offset by 2px `#FFFFFF`).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button**: Solid `#0F2942` background, text in `#FFFFFF`, font `Inter` SemiBold (`label-md`). Hover elevates to `#1E6091` with a seamless 150ms transition. Active press depresses by 1px scale or darkens to `#0B1E30`.
- **Accent Button**: Solid `#2563EB` background with `#FFFFFF` text for immediate conversions and critical workflow catalysts.
- **Secondary (Outline)**: Transparent background, 1px border `#CBD5E1`, text `#0F2942`. Hover changes background to `#F1F5F9` and border to `#94A3B8`.
- **Ghost/Tertiary**: Text `#475569`, no border, transparent background. Hover transitions to `#F1F5F9` with text `#0F172A`.

### Cards & Data Panels
- Surface: `#FFFFFF` surrounded by a `1px solid #E2E8F0` border, with `roundedness: 2` (8px-12px).
- Internal layout: Structured header section separated by a soft horizontal rule (`#F1F5F9`), containing a title in `Plus Jakarta Sans` SemiBold and an optional technical meta-badge.
- Hover states on interactive cards: Subtle border color transition to `#2563EB` and elevation to Layer 2.

### Input Fields
- Height: Standard 40px (compact 32px for high-density tables).
- Surface: `#FFFFFF`, border: `1px solid #CBD5E1`, border-radius: 8px.
- Typography: `#0F172A` text, placeholder `#94A3B8`.
- Focus State: Border color switches to `#2563EB` accompanied by an ambient focus ring: `0 0 0 3px rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.15)`.
- Error State: Border color `#EF4444`, with a supportive icon and error caption in `body-sm`.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Badges**: Subdued semantic tinting. Success uses `#ECFDF5` surface with `#059669` text; Warning uses `#FFFBEB` with `#D97706` text; Critical uses `#FEF2F2` with `#DC2626` text.
- **Category Filter Chips**: `#F1F5F9` surface, `#475569` text, `0.375rem` radius. Selected state transitions to `#0F2942` surface with `#FFFFFF` text.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Custom square (checkbox: 4px radius) and circle (radio) frames measuring 18px x 18px.
- Unchecked: `1.5px solid #94A3B8` on `#FFFFFF`.
- Checked: `#2563EB` solid fill with a crisp `#FFFFFF` checkmark or inner dot.

### Lists & Data Rows
- Striping: Alternating rows utilize `#FFFFFF` and `#F8FAFC`.
- Divider lines: `1px solid #F1F5F9`.
- Row hover: Smooth tint transition to `rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.04)` across the entire row width to guide horizontal eye-tracking across enterprise data columns.

### Enterprise Additions
- **KPI Stat Cards**: Prominent `headline-md` metrics in `#0F2942` topped with an `Inter` uppercase micro-label (`label-sm`) in `#64748B`, paired with a compact trend delta indicator (green/red pill).
- **Executive Breadcrumbs**: Compact horizontal pathway using `/` delimiters in `#94A3B8`, allowing rapid navigation through nested corporate business units.
