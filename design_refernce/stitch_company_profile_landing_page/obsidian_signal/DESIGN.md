## Brand & Style

The design system projects mission-critical reliability, technical precision, and executive authority. Crafted for enterprise security leaders (CISOs, DevSecOps leads, and technical architects), the visual environment abandons cliché neon hacker motifs in favor of institutional-grade composure.

The visual style blends **Corporate Modernism** with **Engineered Minimalism**:
- **Absolute Clarity:** Stark contrasts between abyssal base layers and ultra-bright signals guarantee immediate cognitive intake during threat analysis and product evaluation.
- **Architectural Rigor:** Monolithic layouts, calibrated hairline structural boundaries, and disciplined content distribution evoke military-grade software appliances.
- **Controlled Luminescence:** Cyan and emerald chromatic accents operate exclusively as semantic telemetry signals and high-priority directional indicators, never as gratuitous decorative fills.

## Colors

The palette relies on deep oceanic blacks, sharp architectural slates, pure white typographic punch, and calibrated signal optics.

### Surface Tiers
- **Void Surface (`#0B0D10`):** Core canvas background. Deep, light-absorbing base.
- **Appliance Surface (`#11141A`):** Foundational card and container fill.
- **Elevated Interactive (`#161A22`):** Hover states, elevated panels, modal dialogs, and active toolbars.

### Structural Borders & Hairlines
- **Subtle Rail (`#282E3A`):** Standard card borders, structural grid dividing lines, and inactive input outlines.
- **High-Contrast Stroke (`#4B5563`):** Active structural boundaries and interactive focus dividers.
- **Silver Sheen (`#E5E7EB`):** High-impact chrome highlights, subtle 1px top-rim gradient reflections, and tactile boundary caps.

### Signal Accents
- **Cyan Vector (`#00F2FE`):** Primary action triggers, network link paths, primary status badges, and scanning nodes.
- **Emerald Pulse (`#10B981`):** Verified trust indicators, zero-vulnerability confirmations, uptime health markers.
- **Warning Alert (`#F59E0B`) & Critical Threat (`#EF4444`):** Strict semantic telemetry for breach alerts and threat posture anomalies.

### Typography & Ink
- **Stark Primary (`#FFFFFF`):** High-contrast headlines, critical metrics, and active label text.
- **Muted Slate (`#94A3B8`):** Body paragraphs, contextual metadata, and secondary descriptors.
- **Subdued Wire (`#64748B`):** Ghost captions, disabled states, and table column headers.

## Typography

Typographic execution emphasizes crisp hierarchy, zero ambiguity, and high technical density:
- **Headlines (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Geometric, solid, and authoritative. Tight negative letter-spacing eliminates optical flabbiness across monumental scale titles.
- **Body & Prose (Inter):** Highly neutral and legible across extreme contrast shifts. Optimized for sustained technical whitepapers and documentation scanning.
- **Data & Telemetry (JetBrains Mono):** Reserved for system readouts, IP blocks, hash digests, code representations, and uppercase micro-tags. Imparts an authentic operational console aesthetic.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout system enforces an intentional rhythm combining spacious architectural canvases with dense, high-efficiency telemetry components:

- **Grid Architecture:** 12-column dynamic fluid grid with a maximum content containment cap of `1280px` (`80rem`) for primary layout tracks, scaling up to `1440px` for data-heavy dashboard mocks.
- **Section Rhythm:** Major landing page sections breathe with deliberate separation (minimum `6rem` to `8rem` vertical padding on desktop, consolidating to `3.5rem` on mobile devices).
- **Responsive Adaptations:**
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout; gutter `1.5rem`; canvas margin `1.25rem`. Complex telemetry cards flatten into sequential vertical flows.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8-column layout; gutter `1.5rem`; canvas margin `2.5rem`.
  - **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column layout; gutter `2rem`; canvas margin `4rem`. Asymmetric multi-column spreads paired with anchored telemetry cards.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects blurry, unmotivated drop shadows in favor of **structural containment, low-contrast razor outlines, and ambient dark emission**:

- **Layer 0 (Base Void):** Pure dark background (`#0B0D10`).
- **Layer 1 (Machined Chassis):** Surface fill (`#11141A`) encased in a 1px solid border (`#282E3A`). No ambient drop shadow; structural definition is purely line-based.
- **Layer 2 (Active Telemetry Card):** Surface fill (`#161A22`), encased in a dual-stroke simulation: a 1px border (`#4B5563`) reinforced with an ultra-subtle top internal highlight (`rgba(229, 231, 235, 0.08)`).
- **Layer 3 (Modal / Threat Intercept):** Elevated surface overlay (`#161A22`) enclosed in high-contrast stroke (`#E5E7EB` at 20% opacity), supported by a directional black floor shadow (`0 24px 48px -12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.85)`).
- **Signal Radiance:** Action points utilize an ambient 12px diffuse glow strictly matched to the active signal color (e.g., `0 0 16px rgba(0, 242, 254, 0.25)` for primary focus actions).

## Shapes

The design uses a restrained **Soft (`1`)** shape system to maintain an engineered, tactical posture. 

- **Primary Components (Inputs, Buttons, Cards):** `rounded` (0.25rem / 4px) provides clean structural corners that feel precise and technical without being uncomfortably harsh.
- **Parent Panels & Dashboard Modules:** `rounded-lg` (0.5rem / 8px) frames full sections cleanly while keeping layouts grounded.
- **System Tags & Signal Badges:** Maintained strictly at `rounded` (4px) to preserve data-sheet aesthetics; round pill shapes are explicitly avoided to prevent casual, consumer-app connotations.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Cyber Action:** Stark white (`#FFFFFF`) or high-contrast cyan (`#00F2FE`) solid background with pitch-black (`#0B0D10`) bold typography. Corner radius 4px. Subtle cyan halo on hover (`box-shadow: 0 0 20px rgba(0, 242, 254, 0.35)`).
- **Secondary Shell:** Deep background (`#11141A`), 1px structural border (`#282E3A`), crisp white text. Transitions to border (`#E5E7EB`) on pointer hover.
- **Ghost Utility:** Borderless slate link button with high-contrast underline appearing on hover alongside an inline micro-chevron.

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- Background surface `#11141A` bounded by a 1px `#282E3A` border. Placeholder text in `#64748B`.
- On focus: Active hairline border flashes to `#00F2FE` with a 1px inset boundary, accompanied by a monospace label tag indicator in the upper-right corner.

### Cards & Telemetry Displays
- Base container `#11141A` encased in `#282E3A`.
- Header zones feature an optional hairline divider separating telemetry indicators from card body text.
- Micro dot-grid or structural coordinate marks (`+`) at panel intersections reinforce technical sophistication.

### Badges & Status Chips
- Height 24px, monospace label font, 4px corners.
- **Secured / Valid:** Emerald-tinted surface (`rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.08)`), emerald border (`rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.3)`), emerald text (`#10B981`) paired with a 6px static beacon dot.
- **Active Scan / Monitoring:** Cyan-tinted surface (`rgba(0, 242, 254, 0.08)`), cyan border (`rgba(0, 242, 254, 0.3)`), cyan text (`#00F2FE`).

### Form Checkboxes & Toggles
- Checkboxes: 16x16px square with 2px radius. Unchecked state uses `#161A22` with `#4B5563` perimeter. Checked state flips to solid `#00F2FE` with a pitch-black `#0B0D10` checkmark.
- Switches: Industrial rectangular toggle housing with a crisp sliding indicator block.

### Additional Domain Components
- **Threat Pulse Ticker:** Monospace stream displaying real-time inspection latencies, blocked attacks, and active firewall states.
- **Network Architecture Nodes:** High-contrast interconnect blocks linked by 1px directional hairpins that switch between static slate and luminous cyan.
